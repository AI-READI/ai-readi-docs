title: Azure Storage Overview
---

Due to the size of the AI-READI dataset, we require requestors to provide an Azure storage container to receive the processed data. Specifically, at the end of the request process you will need to provide:

1. Storage Account Name
1. Blob Container Name
1. Blob SAS Token (with "Write" permission only)

Follow the steps over the next few pages to create your Azure storage environment. If your research is Type-2-Diabetes-related, please do this before you attempt to request the AI-READI dataset as it can lead to delays in processing your request.

**If** your request will require a [Data Transfer and Use Agreement](https://docs.aireadi.org/docs/3/preliminary/license#which-agreement-to-use), i.e. for non-diabetes-related research, you may consider deferring the creation of your Azure Storage environment. Non-diabetes studies require DTUAs and must be approved by the Data Access Committee. If you wait to create the Azure account and container until after your study has been approved, you will be able to utilize Azure's free 100GB download promotion tied to the first month of storage. You can still submit your request now without Azure storage details.
